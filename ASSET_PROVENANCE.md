# AI teaching illustrations

24 project-bound illustrations were generated with the built-in image generator and optimised as WebP under `public/learning/`. They are fictional teaching scenes, not documentary proof, actual employee photographs or product screenshots.

Prompt brief: wide landscape enterprise editorial illustration, natural human proportions, recognisable Indian business context, soft daylight, warm cream, forest green, sage and muted ochre. No words, logos, watermarks or exact invented data. Each scene makes a concrete learning decision visible.

Scene prompts: group leadership training; onboarding document review; safe manufacturing shift handover; software demonstration; Dubai cross-border project coordination; practical community skills; asset return inspection; respectful policy discussion; attendance discrepancy resolution; India shift planning; future opportunity through learning; integrity when handling a confidential folder; payroll second review; qualified digital enquiries; editorial fact-checking; employer/vacancy verification; reading a declaration; respectful exit handover; private employee request; obtaining required PPE; listening to a demo objection; replacing personal records with synthetic demo data; transport disruption coordination; technical support handoff.

The full scene-to-lesson mapping and learner-facing captions are in `lib/hub-examples.ts`. Exact business flowcharts are rendered in code and are not generated images.
